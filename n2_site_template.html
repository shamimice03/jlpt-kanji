Sentence furigana is generated with the UniDic analyser and overridden with the verified vocabulary readings. Progress is saved in this browser only — nothing is uploaded anywhere.

The badge beside each word is the JLPT vocabulary list it appears on, which is separate from the kanji's own level — 政党 is an N2 word written with an N2 kanji, 与党 an N1 word written with the same one. A dashed — means the word is on no JLPT vocabulary list at all; 党員 and 党首 are ordinary Japanese, the lists simply don't carry them. About a third of the vocabulary here is unlisted in that way.
